Honest FAQ

Does deleting ScrollBreeze remove the protection?

Yes — and every screen-time app works this way, most just won't say it. Deleting ScrollBreeze removes the shield. We'd rather earn your trust than hide the limits.

Can I cheat the breathing?

Technically you can tap through it. But the benefit of breathing belongs to you — cheating only cheats your own dopamine. We designed ScrollBreeze assuming you're on your own side.

What happens to my task photos?

They live in memory for a few seconds while AI checks them, then they're gone. Never stored, never uploaded to our servers, never used for training.
